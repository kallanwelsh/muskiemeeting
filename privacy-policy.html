01What we collect

When you book a room with Muskie Meeting through our Cal.com booking page, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, mobile phone number, and the dates and times of your stay. We don't collect anything beyond what's needed to give you access to your room.

02How we use it

We use your booking details to generate a temporary access code for the smart lock on your room, and we use your phone number for exactly one purpose: to text you that code, along with your check-in window and directions to your room, shortly after you book.

03Sharing & SMS data

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to deliver the automated messages described in this policy, and never for promotional messages from us or anyone else.

Your phone number and booking details are processed by our booking platform (Cal.com), our smart-lock provider (igloohome), and our text-messaging provider (Twilio) strictly to operate this service — never sold or shared beyond what's required to deliver your access code.

04Message frequency & rates

You will receive one (1) text message per booking, sent automatically once your booking is confirmed. We do not send recurring or marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

05Retention

We keep booking and contact information only as long as needed to support your stay and to meet basic recordkeeping and legal requirements, after which it's deleted.

06Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message from us to stop receiving texts.
  • Reply HELP to any message for assistance.
  • To ask us to delete your information, reply to your booking confirmation or contact us through the Cal.com booking platform.

07Security

Access codes are generated per booking and expire automatically at the end of your stay window. We rely on our booking, lock, and messaging providers' standard security practices to protect your information in transit and at rest.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to book with us after a change means you accept the updated policy.
