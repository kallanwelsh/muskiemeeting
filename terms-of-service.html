01The program

Muskie Meeting sends one automated text message per confirmed booking, containing your room's temporary access code, your check-in and check-out times, and directions to your room at 104 W Comanche Ave, Shabbona, IL. This is a transactional service message, not a marketing program.

02How you opt in

You opt in by entering your mobile phone number as part of completing a booking on our Cal.com booking page. Submitting a booking with a phone number is your consent to receive the one-time text described above for that booking.

03Frequency & cost

One message per booking. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
